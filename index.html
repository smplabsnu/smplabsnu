Research Areas

Our current investigations span theory, experiment, and hardware design.

Nanoscale Interfacial Phenomena

Investigating the behavior of functionalized iron nanoparticles at water-toluene interfaces. We also focus on the stabilization mechanics of Polyhedral Oligomeric Silsesquioxanes (POSS) on Indium Tin Oxide (ITO) glass substrates for advanced coating applications.

Theoretical & Statistical Mechanics

Deep mathematical explorations into quantum systems. Our recent focus includes time-dependent harmonic perturbation theory and rigorous investigations surrounding the conditions and proofs of the ergodic hypothesis in complex dynamical systems.

Hardware & Instrumentation

Bridging the gap between theory and physical measurement through custom electronics. We design and prototype specialized lab circuits utilizing operational amplifiers and integrated circuits to capture low-noise signals in sensitive environments.
